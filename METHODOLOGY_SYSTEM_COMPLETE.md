## 🔍 Key Findings & Corrections

### Critical Technical Correction
**BEFORE (incorrect):**
> "Workspace files reset on restart"

**AFTER (correct per Replit docs):**
> "Files in your Replit App's workspace, including application code, static assets, and configuration files, are **persisted across sessions**."  
> *(Source: [Replit Apps - File Storage](https://docs.replit.com/hosting/deployments/about-deployments#storage))*

### Important Distinction Clarified
- ✅ **Workspace files** (code, docs, scripts) → Persistent during development
- ❌ **Deployment filesystem** → NOT persistent after republish
- 💡 **Solution:** PostgreSQL backup provides extra protection layer
