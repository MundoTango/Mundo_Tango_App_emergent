# Privacy Policy - Mundo Tango

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2025  
**Effective Date:** [Launch Date]

## 1. Introduction

Mundo Tango ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Name, email, username, password, profile photo
- **Profile Data:** Bio, location (city/country), tango experience, preferences
- **User Content:** Posts, photos, videos, comments, messages, event RSVPs
- **Payment Information:** Credit card details (processed securely by Stripe)
- **Communications:** Support requests, feedback, survey responses

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, features used, time spent, click patterns
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device ID
- **Location Data:** Approximate location based on IP (not precise GPS)
- **Cookies:** Authentication, preferences, analytics (see Cookie Policy)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **OAuth Providers:** If you sign in with Replit/Google/etc., we receive your email and basic profile
- **Payment Processors:** Transaction status and payment confirmation from Stripe
- **Analytics Services:** Aggregated usage data from PostHog, Plausible, Sentry

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- **Provide the Service:** Account management, content delivery, messaging
- **Personalize Experience:** Recommendations, relevant content, language preferences
- **Process Payments:** Subscriptions, event registrations, marketplace transactions
- **Communicate:** Service updates, newsletters, support responses
- **Improve the Service:** Analytics, bug fixes, feature development
- **Ensure Security:** Fraud prevention, abuse detection, authentication
- **Comply with Law:** Legal obligations, law enforcement requests

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Public Information
The following information is publicly visible:
- Public profile (name, bio, location, tango experience)
- Public posts, comments, and likes
- Event RSVPs (if event is public)
- Group memberships (if group is public)

### 4.2 With Other Users
- Direct messages are visible to recipients
- Private posts are visible to selected users/groups
- Your follow list may be visible to others

### 4.3 With Service Providers
We share data with trusted third parties:
- **Stripe:** Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
- **Sentry:** Error tracking (anonymized)
- **PostHog/Plausible:** Analytics (privacy-focused)
- **OpenAI:** AI features (no training on your data)
- **Replit:** Hosting and infrastructure

### 4.4 For Legal Reasons
We may disclose information to:
- Comply with laws, regulations, or court orders
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Prevent fraud or abuse

### 4.5 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale, your information may be transferred to the new owner.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

### 5.1 Access and Portability
- View your personal information in Account Settings
- Download your data (posts, photos, messages) in JSON format

### 5.2 Correction and Deletion
- Update your profile information at any time
- Delete your account (data retained for 30 days for recovery)

### 5.3 Privacy Controls
- Set posts to public, friends-only, or private
- Control who can see your profile, follows, and activity
- Opt out of marketing emails (unsubscribe link)

### 5.4 GDPR Rights (EU Users)
If you are in the EU, you have additional rights:
- Right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

### 5.5 CCPA Rights (California Users)
If you are in California, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information we collect
- Know whether we sell or share personal information (we do not)
- Opt out of the sale of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:
- Your account is active
- Necessary to provide the Service
- Required by law (e.g., tax records)

**Retention Periods:**
- Active accounts: Indefinitely
- Deleted accounts: 30 days (then permanently deleted)
- Messages: Until deleted by sender/recipient
- Analytics data: 2 years
- Payment records: 7 years (legal requirement)

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- **Encryption:** TLS/SSL for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest
- **Authentication:** bcrypt password hashing, JWT tokens
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access, least privilege principle
- **Monitoring:** Security audits, intrusion detection, error tracking
- **Backups:** Daily automated backups, disaster recovery plan

However, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

### 8.1 Types of Cookies
- **Essential Cookies:** Required for authentication and security
- **Functional Cookies:** Remember your preferences (language, theme)
- **Analytics Cookies:** Track usage patterns (PostHog, Plausible)
- **Performance Cookies:** Monitor errors and performance (Sentry)

### 8.2 Cookie Consent
- Essential cookies are automatically enabled
- You can opt out of analytics cookies in Settings > Privacy
- Disabling cookies may limit functionality

### 8.3 Do Not Track
We honor Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals by disabling analytics.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Mundo Tango is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, contact us to delete it.

## 10. International Data Transfers

- Privacy Shield (US-EU, if applicable)
- Data Processing Agreements with service providers
